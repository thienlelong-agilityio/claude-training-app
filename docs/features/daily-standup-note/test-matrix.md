# Test Matrix: Daily standup note
Source: [spec.md](spec.md) (approved), [tickets/](tickets/) T01, T02, T03. Every ticket's `Open questions` says `None.`.

## Test Plan
- **Scope:** T01 (write, auto-save, edit, independence from completion: R1, R2, R3, R5, R7, R10, R13, R14), T02 (storage notice, sign-in and sign-out: R6, R7, R9, R12), T03 (Copy for Slack and empty-note guard: R4, R8, R10, R11).
- **Environments:** the practice app at `http://localhost:3000` in desktop Chrome (latest) and one other browser (for the cross-browser case); Guest (signed out) and Signed in (practice sign-in); keyboard only; a screen reader (for example NVDA).
- **Data setup:** an empty browser profile; a browser that already holds progress from v1.0 (`{"day-12":true,"day-13":true}` under `training_progress`); boundary notes of 12, 499, 500, 501 and 600 characters; text with line breaks, spaces only, `**bold**`, `<script>alert(1)</script>`, Vietnamese letters and emoji; the word `SENTINEL` placed on the clipboard by hand before copy tests.
- **Top Risks:**
  1. The latest text is lost when the learner leaves, ticks the day or signs in before the 2-second save (TC-16, TC-17, TC-29).
  2. Signing in overwrites a note, or notes are mixed between days (TC-04, TC-26).
  3. The copied text has the wrong header or status, or breaks in Slack (TC-31, TC-32, TC-48).
  4. Existing completed-day progress is changed by the feature (TC-06, TC-28).
  5. A blocked browser feature (saving, clipboard) fails silently (TC-18, TC-35).
- **What's NOT tested:** real cross-device sync (not possible in the practice version, R6); a lead or other learner seeing a note (no such screen exists, R5 is checked in TC-05); the "Saved to your Google account" wording beyond checking it is unchanged (TC-30); sending to Slack by the app (the learner pastes by hand); usage measurement (done by a survey after Day 15, not in the app).

## Removed on purpose
- **Combined:** Day 13 and Day 14 headers and the status check into one Unit case (TC-32); Enter and Space into one case (TC-34); the repeated tick and untick sequences into one case (TC-09); the two shared-browser cases (different name and two people on one browser) into one (TC-27); the empty-note checks on page load and on pressing the button into one (TC-37); the "type text enables, delete disables" pair into one (TC-38); the checks for "shown exactly as typed" for script text, markup, Vietnamese letters and emoji into one (TC-22); the two sign-in, sign-out and 500-character checks into one (TC-26).
- **Removed:** cloud and offline sync cases (the spec keeps notes in the browser, R6); role cases between the lead and other learners (the app has no such roles, R5 is checked once in TC-05); a separate Security group (its cases sit in Negative test and Permission / Data variation); "copy is the same when signed in" (copy does not depend on sign-in, so it cannot fail for that reason).

## Spec Gaps
QA raises these through the tickets (in their `Open questions`), not directly to developers.
- **G1 (T01):** What does the learner see when the saved note cannot be read (damaged data in the browser)? Blank note with no message, an error, or something else? Blocks TC-19.
- **G2 (T01):** When the same day is open in two tabs, which text wins, and does the other tab update? Blocks TC-20.
- **G3 (T01):** How does the counter count emoji and accented letters (one character or more)? Does it say "1 character left" (singular) at one left?
- **G4 (T01):** Does the counter show on page load for a saved note? AC2 only says it updates "when the learner types".
- **G5 (T01, T03):** R10 says confirmation messages are read by a screen reader, but the tickets say so only for "Saved" and "Copied to your clipboard". Are "Your note was shortened to 500 characters.", "Could not save your note…" and "Copy did not work…" also announced?
- **G6 (T01):** When text is pasted into an existing note and the total goes over 500, are the first 500 characters of the whole note kept, or is the pasted text cut to fit?
- **G7 (T03):** Are leading and trailing spaces in the note copied exactly or trimmed? The ticket only says line breaks are kept.
- **G8 (T03):** Does "Copied to your clipboard" (or the failure message) clear when the learner ticks or unticks the day? The tickets say it clears when the note is edited.
